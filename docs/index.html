COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQs

Does it work offline?

Yes. Core records remain available without internet access. You can create jobs, manage customers, record payments and continue daily work. Internet is only needed for features such as updates, online recovery and email links.

How are updates delivered?

New installers are published through GitHub Releases. When you are online, PrintManager checks for a signed release and lets you install it safely without changing your company records.

Can teams share data?

Company network features connect authorized workplace computers. The owner controls access, while each team member sees the tools and information needed for their role.

Can I recover my data?

Use local encrypted backups, a synced folder or Dropbox recovery. Keep your encryption password safe, and verify a backup before relying on it on another computer.

Does it track materials?

Yes. Purchases, stock, metres used and remaining material are connected. When a job uses a selected material, the used quantity is deducted automatically and the remaining stock stays visible.

Who is it for?

PrintManager is built for owners, sales teams, designers, machine operators, accountants and storekeepers, so everyone can work from one reliable record.

How do I set up my company?

Choose Create account, enter your printing company details, add your logo and invite your team. You can update company information later from Settings.

How do I create a job for a customer?

Search for an existing customer in New Print Job, select them, add one or more work items, choose the material and enter the dimensions. The calculator updates the quantity and price before you save.

How are invoices and payments tracked?

Create an invoice from a job and record full or partial payments. PrintManager shows paid, unpaid and remaining balances so follow-ups are easy.

Can I export business reports?

Yes. Cash-flow, profit and operational reports can be exported in the available formats for sharing, review or your accounting records.

Is my business data secure?

Your records are stored locally and protected by your company sign-in. Use strong passwords, role permissions and encrypted backups, and never share recovery files publicly.

What happens if I need help?

Use the Report issue form and include the exact error message, what you were doing and your app version. This gives support enough context to investigate quickly.

DATA RECOVERY

Recover your PrintManager data

Choose the recovery method that matches where your verified backup is stored.

  1. Local backup file: Copy your encrypted backup file to the new computer, open PrintManager, choose Recover existing company, and select the file.
  2. Synced folder: Sign in to the same computer or synced folder account, then select the latest backup copy from the Backup page.
  3. Dropbox backup: Connect the Dropbox account used for backups, choose a dated backup, and enter its encryption password.
  4. Printing database file: If you have a trusted printing.db file, use the database recovery option and keep the original file safely preserved.

Important: Use the same encryption password and never upload backup files to public websites.
